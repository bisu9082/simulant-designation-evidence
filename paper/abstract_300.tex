Chemical warfare agent detection is developed almost entirely on simulants:
compounds accepted as agent-like and safe enough to handle in an ordinary
laboratory. We tested whether that acceptance has a measurable basis. It does
not, and what produces the appearance of one is the composition of the
comparison. For eleven authentic agents we computed structural and GFN2-xTB
electronic descriptors and asked, by exact permutation over every possible
designation, whether three incumbent simulants sit closer to the agents than
compounds drawn at random. Our primary panel of thirty-five compounds includes
five further compounds the sensor, sorbent and decontamination literature also
uses as simulants; a twenty-eight-compound panel omitting them is reported
alongside, because the contrast is the result. On the primary panel no agent has
a valid match at any threshold, and no test in three descriptor spaces under six
distance statistics falls below $p=0.15$. Resampling the descriptors gives the
incumbent three a median of zero matched agents against five for the eight the
field uses, the difference positive in 1000 of 1000 replicates. The distances
barely move between panels; what changes is that something closer is present.
Diisopropyl fluorophosphate displaces dimethyl methylphosphonate for sarin,
soman and cyclosarin, and 2-chloroethyl ethyl sulfide sits at 0.57 typical
separations from sulfur mustard against dichloromethane's 1.67. The smaller
panel yields three matches and a smallest nominal $p$ of 0.025. How that is
corrected decides the answer, so we report five schemes, under which nothing
reaches 0.05 except a statistic specified in advance ($q=0.042$), which we did
not specify. On the safety axis dimethyl methylphosphonate has no
regulator-derived acute inhalation value at all. The documentary record
supplies the mechanism: two of the three
compounds were designated for measurements that are not detection chemistry, and
for the third we found no designating instrument. A defensible simulant claim
must name the originating measurement and fix the panel, the space and the
statistic before the test is run.
